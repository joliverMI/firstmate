--- The Admiral's view before the work lands (bin/fm-dashboard.sh show $card) ---
    title:    Card held at needs_attention (OLD teardown)
    status:   needs_attention
    agent:    
    starred:  false
    needs attention: approve the $400 vendor renewal
    
    --- prompt ---
    demo
    

--- OLD behaviour: pre-fix fm-teardown.sh ran exactly this on landing ---
    $ bin/fm-dashboard.sh status card-held-at-needs-attention-old-teardow-ki6e review
    card's own status-history entry for that transition:
      to_status=review  note=[]
    needs_attention_reason column now: []
    >>> the answer to what the card was held for is GONE from the record entirely.

--- NEW behaviour: fm_dashboard_advance_after_landing (the shared helper teardown now calls) ---
    $ fm_dashboard_advance_after_landing <dash> card-held-at-needs-attention-new-teardow-1ezd demo-task review "<audit msg>"
    dashboard: advanced card card-held-at-needs-attention-new-teardow-1ezd to review for demo-task
    card's own status-history entry for that transition:
      to_status=review  note=[approve the $400 vendor renewal]
    needs_attention_reason column now: []
    >>> the reason survived the transition, as the note on the very transition that cleared the column.

--- Where the operator can and cannot see it (honest scope) ---
    retrievable:     $ bin/fm-dashboard.sh show card-held-at-needs-attention-new-teardow-1ezd --json | jq -r '.status_history[-1].note'
                     approve the $400 vendor renewal
    NOT surfaced by: bin/fm-dashboard.sh show card-held-at-needs-attention-new-teardow-1ezd   (plain output renders no status history):
      title:    Card held at needs_attention (NEW teardown)
      status:   review
      agent:    
      starred:  false
      
      --- prompt ---
      demo
      
    ...and the web board renders no status history either. Moved from silently
    destroyed to silently preserved where nobody can see it yet - follow-up work.
